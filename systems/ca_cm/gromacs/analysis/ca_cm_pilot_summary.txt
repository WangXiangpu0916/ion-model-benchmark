Ca CM hydration pilot: summary
Record date: 2026-11-02
Status: one completed 2 ns pilot trajectory with basic analyses.

SYSTEM AND METHODS
- Composition: 1 Ca2+, 2 Cl-, 859 waters; 2580 atoms; total charge 0.
- Models: Li-Merz CM Ca2+, standard Amber TIP3P, Joung-Cheatham Cl-.
- Parameter files: frcmod.ions234lm_126_tip3p and frcmod.ionsjc_tip3p.
- Matched build: systems/ca_cm/amber/build_matched.leap.
- Amber inputs for conversion: system_matched.prmtop and system_matched.inpcrd.
- The initial 897-water Ca build was not used for this matched comparison.
- Active simulation inputs: systems/ca_cm/gromacs/system.top and system.gro.
- Engine: GROMACS 2025.4; topology conversion with ParmEd.
- Minimization: 886 steps; final maximum force below 100 kJ/mol/nm.
- Early minimization SETTLE warnings occurred; subsequent dynamics completed without detected numerical warnings.
- Preparation: 20 ps NVT followed by 1000 ps NPT.
- Production: 2 ns NPT, 1 fs timestep, 298 K, 1 bar.
- Coupling: V-rescale thermostat and isotropic C-rescale barostat.
- Production ld-seed: 20261113; velocities inherited from equilibration.
- Trajectory: 2001 frames spanning 0-2000 ps, saved every 1 ps.

- Independent repeats and IOD parameter comparisons remain pending.
