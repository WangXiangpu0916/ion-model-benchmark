SYSTEM AND METHODS
- Composition: 1 Ca2+, 2 Cl-, 859 waters; 2580 atoms; total charge 0.
- Models: Li-Merz CM Ca2+, standard Amber TIP3P, Joung-Cheatham Cl-.
- Parameter files: data/raw/frcmod.ions234lm_126_tip3p and data/raw/frcmod.ionsjc_tip3p.
- Original matched build: systems/ca_cm/amber/build_matched.leap; ParmEd conversion used system_matched.prmtop and system_matched.inpcrd.
- The initial 897-water Ca build was not used for this matched comparison.
- Active repeat inputs: systems/ca_cm/rep03/gromacs/system.top and start.gro.
- system.top is byte-identical to the original Ca topology; start.gro is byte-identical to systems/ca_cm/gromacs/em.gro.
- The original Ca minimization converged in 886 steps with maximum force below 100 kJ/mol/nm. Early SETTLE warnings belong to that preparation history; minimization was not repeated for replicate 03.
- Replicates 01, 02, and 03 share the same minimized starting configuration. New initial velocities and stochastic seeds were used for replicate 03.
- Engine: GROMACS 2025.4, /opt/gromacs-2025.4-cuda/bin/gmx.
- Preparation: 20 ps NVT, then 200 ps NPT continued from its checkpoint to a total of 1000 ps.
- Production: 2 ns NPT, 1 fs timestep, 298 K, 1 bar; production time resets to 0 ps after equilibration.
- Coupling: V-rescale thermostat, tau-t 0.1 ps; isotropic C-rescale barostat, tau-p 5.0 ps.
- Electrostatics: PME with 1.0 nm real-space cutoff. Lennard-Jones: 1.0 nm cutoff, Potential-shift, DispCorr = EnerPres.
- Water rigidity is specified by SETTLE in the topology.
- NVT gen-seed and ld-seed: 20261221; NPT ld-seed: 20261222; production ld-seed: 20261223.
- NVT generates initial velocities at 298 K; NPT and production inherit the preceding state without generating new velocities.
- Production trajectory: 2001 frames spanning 0-2000 ps, saved every 1 ps.
- Energy records: 20001 points spanning 0-2000 ps, saved every 0.1 ps.
- NVT, NPT, and production completed normally without detected numerical warnings. Production reached step 2000000 at 2000 ps.

LIMITATIONS AND REMAINING WORK
- These results describe the third Ca CM trajectory, using the same starting configuration as replicates 01 and 02.
- Different seeds do not establish independence from the shared starting configuration or complete sampling of slow processes.
- Similar RDFs across repeats or trajectory halves do not establish full convergence.
- Sampling every 1 ps cannot resolve all brief departures and returns.
- No reliable water-exchange rate or robust between-replicate uncertainty has been estimated.
- The three planned CM seeds for each ion are complete; IOD parameter comparisons remain pending.
- A complete cross-engine periodic-system energy/force comparison remains unfinished.
- These water-box results do not establish NMDAR binding, permeation, or blockade mechanisms.
