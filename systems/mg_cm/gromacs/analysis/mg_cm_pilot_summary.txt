SYSTEM AND METHODS
- Composition: 1 Mg2+, 2 Cl-, and 859 water molecules; 2580 atoms; total charge 0.
- Parameter assignments, as labeled in the local parameter files: Li-Merz CM Mg2+, standard Amber TIP3P water, and Joung-Cheatham Cl-.
- Local parameter files used: data/raw/frcmod.ions234lm_126_tip3p and data/raw/frcmod.ionsjc_tip3p.
- Construction: AmberTools tleap, followed by ParmEd conversion to GROMACS.
- Build input: systems/mg_cm/amber/build.leap.
- Active GROMACS topology and starting coordinates: systems/mg_cm/gromacs/system.top and system.gro.
- Simulation engine recorded in the logs: GROMACS 2025.4, /opt/gromacs-2025.4-cuda/bin/gmx.
- Energy minimization reached Fmax < 100 kJ mol^-1 nm^-1 after 1097 steps.
- Preparation: 20 ps NVT, followed by NPT extended from 200 ps to a total of 1000 ps.
- Production: 2 ns NPT; timestep 0.001 ps; target temperature 298 K; target pressure 1 bar.
- Temperature coupling: V-rescale, tau-t 0.1 ps. Pressure coupling: isotropic C-rescale, tau-p 5.0 ps.
- Electrostatics: PME, real-space cutoff 1.0 nm. Lennard-Jones: cutoff 1.0 nm, Potential-shift, DispCorr = EnerPres.
- Water rigidity is specified by SETTLE in the topology; constraints = none does not imply flexible water.
- Production inherits the preceding state without generating new velocities; ld-seed = 20261103.
- Production trajectory: one saved frame every 1 ps, covering 0-2000 ps, for 2001 frames.
- The production log reaches step 2000000 at 2000 ps and records normal completion. No numerical warnings were found in the inspected production logs.
- Early minimization SETTLE warnings were reported during preparation; these should remain part of the project history.
- The 'Fixed volume' comment in md.mdp is outdated: the effective settings enable C-rescale pressure coupling, so production is NPT.

INTERPRETATION AND REMAINING WORK
- These observations describe one 2 ns Mg CM pilot trajectory, not an independently replicated comparison.
- Similar RDFs in two segments of the same trajectory do not establish full convergence or provide independent replicates.
- Unchanged water identities at 1 ps sampling intervals do not exclude brief departures and returns between frames and do not support a reliable water-exchange rate estimate.
- Stable-looking thermodynamic or structural curves do not establish convergence of all slow processes.
- A complete cross-engine periodic-system single-point energy and force comparison remains unfinished.
- Ca CM, independent replicates, and IOD parameter comparisons remain pending.
- This water-box pilot does not establish ion binding, permeation, or blockade mechanisms inside NMDAR.
- Planned next step: review the Mg build input, recorded conversion procedure, and effective simulation settings before preparing a Ca CM control with the same water model and comparable conditions.
